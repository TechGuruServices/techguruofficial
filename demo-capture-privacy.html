Privacy Policy — DemoGlide by TECHGURU

Effective date: October 15, 2026

TECHGURU Studio is a solo web design studio run by its owner and developer. I built the DemoGlide by TECHGURU Chrome extension ("the extension") as a tool that captures a 10–30 second full-viewport site tour (video + screenshots in a single .zip) of the current browser tab. This policy explains what data the extension handles.

How the extension works

The extension does nothing until you click "Capture this tab". It then takes screenshots of that one tab, stitches them into a short tour video with automatic quality gates (the video encoding runs in a hidden processing document inside your browser), packages the video and screenshots into a .zip, and saves it to your device using the browser's download API. Every step runs locally in your browser.

Data I do not collect

  • No account information or sign-in data
  • No browsing history — nothing is captured on tabs you don't explicitly start a tour on
  • No analytics, cookies, or tracking of any kind
  • No data stored remotely — I operate no server that receives extension data
  • No data sold to or shared with third parties

The finished .zip is saved only to your own device. Nothing the extension handles is transmitted anywhere.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • activeTab — capture screenshots of the tab you explicitly start a tour on. This is the only host access the extension needs; it requests no broad host permissions.
  • scripting — inject the tour planner that scrolls the page between tour stops.
  • downloads — save the finished .zip package to your device.
  • offscreen — run a hidden processing document that encodes the tour video. The browser's video encoder is only available in document contexts, so this step cannot run in the extension's background service worker.

Third-party services

The extension does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or analytics providers. Its video and zip libraries (mp4-muxer, fflate) are bundled inside the extension itself — it makes no network calls at runtime.

Children's privacy

The extension is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect data from children.

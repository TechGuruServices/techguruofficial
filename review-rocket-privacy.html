Privacy Policy — TECHGURU Review Rocket

Effective date: October 9, 2026

TECHGURU Studio ("we", "us") built the TECHGURU Review Rocket Chrome extension ("the extension") as a free tool for field contractors to generate one-click review request messages. This policy explains what data the extension handles.

Data stored on your device

The extension stores the following only in your browser's local extension storage (Chrome's storage API), so you don't have to re-enter it each time:

  • Your business name
  • Your Google review link

This data never leaves your device. We do not operate any server that receives extension data, and the extension does not transmit your information anywhere.

Data we do not collect

  • No account information or sign-in data
  • No browsing history or website content
  • No analytics, cookies, or tracking of any kind
  • No data sold to or shared with third parties

The extension requests only the storage permission. It does not read your tabs, browsing activity, or any other browser data.

Third-party services

The extension does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or analytics providers.

Children's privacy

The extension is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this URL.

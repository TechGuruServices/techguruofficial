New to browser extensions?

You're 60 seconds away.

TECHGURU extensions are lightweight add-ons for your computer's Chrome or Edge browser (Windows, Mac, or Chromebook). Three steps, no new system to learn:

1Add itfrom its store
2Pin itso it's always one tap away
3Use iton the job

1. Add it. On Review Rocket above, click Install Free — Chrome, then Add to Chrome, or Install Free — Edge, then Get in Microsoft Edge. On TerraScope, click Install Free, then Add to Chrome. For Estimate Closer, click Install Free above, then Get in Microsoft Edge. Done.

2. Pin it. Click the puzzle-piece Extensions icon in your browser's top-right (Chrome or Edge), find the tool, click Pin. It lives in your toolbar from then on.

3. Use it. Review Rocket: tap the pinned icon after the job and send the review request on the spot. TerraScope: draw your boundary on its satellite map — area, volume, and cost update as you draw.

Heads up: these run in desktop Chrome or Edge only — not on phones or tablets. (Your customers' review links open anywhere.)
